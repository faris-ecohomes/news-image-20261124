# news-image-20261124 — Tuesday 24 November 2026 in one picture

A newspaper front page that sums up Tuesday 24 November 2026 in a single
1024×1024 image: 54 stories, all verified from named sources and dated to
that day.

## What the picture shows

- **16 numbered panels** with a small illustration, a region and topic tag,
  a headline, a short deck, and a source-status chip (CONFIRMED or
  REPORTED) — from the Ulleung air-defence alert and the Polish drone
  incursion to GTA VI's 45-million-copy launch weekend and the Beaver
  Supermoon.
- **A UTC timeline** of the day as reported, from the 00:02 Tickner
  injury news to the 14:53 supermoon peak.
- **"Where it happened"** — a dotted world map with 14 numbered pins.
- **"Also on Tuesday 24 November"** — 38 more verified stories in brief.
- **A DATE CHECK box** listing the big stories deliberately left out
  because they broke before 24 November (Nvidia reinstating Huang, the
  Megaspeed blacklisting, the COP31 collapse, the GTA VI launch itself)
  and the ones still to come (Germany's Ifo index, US consumer
  confidence, South Korea's rate decision).

Every story was cross-checked against its wire dateline, not an
aggregator timestamp: several stories that looked like 24 November news
were in fact from 22–23 November and were moved to the DATE CHECK box.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py
# writes /tmp/out/entry.png (1024x1024 PNG, under 1 MiB)
```

`main.py` uses only the Python standard library. It copies the poster
`entry.png` that ships in this repository to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` and
verifies the PNG header is 1024×1024 first.

## How it was made

The poster was drawn in the build session by a fresh Python/Pillow
generator (not shipped in this repository) from a story list researched
that same day. The generator, the story list and the render checks live
outside the repo; what ships here is only the finished image and the
copy program above.
